Featured Project

The Triad of Modern Cinema

Movie Studio Data Visualization Website

Introduction to Visual Analytics January–April 2026 Team Project

An interactive data visualization website that compares Marvel, DC, and Pixar through box-office revenue, audience ratings, popularity, and genre trends.

The project translates a large movie dataset into an accessible visual story, helping users identify patterns in the performance and cultural influence of the three studios.

My contributions

  • Analyzed critique feedback and identified the most important usability and visual-design improvements.
  • Improved colour consistency across studio visualizations, making Marvel, DC, and Pixar easier to distinguish.
  • Increased typography and chart sizes to improve readability and make important findings more noticeable.
  • Helped refine annotations and supporting explanations so users could better understand changes in revenue and performance.
  • Contributed to the interface structure and front-end presentation of the final website.
